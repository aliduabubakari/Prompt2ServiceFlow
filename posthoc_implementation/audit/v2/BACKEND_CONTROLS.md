## Backend-neutral business checks

Both conditions receive the same checker. It checks actual business metrics,
duplicate/window counts, published CSV values, and publication/source integrity
against the previously developed independent Decimal/window oracle calculated
from actual public inputs. It does not require an engine's internal trace,
stage names, or profile-override log to pass. An exception or missing business
output is recorded as a failed business check, not an alternative success flag.

Before running the mock condition, require the checker to accept all verified
real positive snapshots and reject four deliberately changed real snapshots:
constant summary, wrong energy, wrong alert, and missing publication. Where
values change, recompute the CSV checksum so detection cannot rely only on a
stale hash. These mutants are isolated negative controls, never repaired or
reported model outputs. The three expected-invalid-input scenarios were already
tested in the earlier real-profile controls; this contrast uses the six positive
scenarios to avoid equating arbitrary process failure with a correct rejection.

No inference, no original model workflow execution, and no original-score or
paper changes. This is one mechanism-control case, not broad semantic success
or proof that all 110 tasks can run under this profile. Other operations require
