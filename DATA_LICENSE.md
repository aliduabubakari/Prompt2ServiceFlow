# Data licensing and attribution

The software license does not replace the licenses of source datasets.

The benchmark source layer records the provenance, checksums, and licensing information used to construct the corpus. The source dataset titled *Structured ETL and Data-Preparation Pipeline Descriptions for LLM Workflow Generation Studies* is recorded under CC BY 4.0 in `benchmark/source/zenodo_v1_0_0/SOURCE.json`; see that directory and `benchmark/source/internal_specs/PROVENANCE.md` for the exact provenance statements.

Generated experiment outputs and derived result tables are provided for scholarly verification of the accompanying paper. Users who redistribute source-derived benchmark content must preserve its attribution and comply with its source license.
